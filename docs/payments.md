# Fees and payments

## Application fee

Keystone charges a flat $29 application fee per application. The fee covers identity verification and the credit and background reports shared with the landlord.

## Refunds

The fee is refundable only if the application expires without the landlord ever opening it. Refunds are issued to the original card within 5 to 7 business days. Declined applications are not refunded, since the reports were already generated.

## Payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, and American Express. We do not accept debit cards issued outside the US, prepaid cards, or bank transfers.

## Receipts

Receipts are emailed on payment and are also available under Settings > Billing.
