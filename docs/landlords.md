# Landlords

## Receiving applications

Landlords create a listing and share the listing link. Applications for that listing appear on the landlord dashboard with the tenant's verification, credit, and background reports.

## Responding

Landlords should approve or decline within 3 business days. After 30 days without a response the application expires and the tenant is refunded automatically.

## Report contents

Reports include a credit score band (not the raw score), eviction history, and criminal background where local law permits. Landlords never see the tenant's Social Security number or card details.

## Pricing for landlords

Keystone is free for landlords. Tenants pay the application fee.
