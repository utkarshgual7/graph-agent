# Account and security

## Password reset

Go to the sign-in page and choose "Forgot password". We send a reset link to the email on the account; the link expires after 15 minutes. If you no longer have access to that email, contact support from any address and we will verify your identity manually.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication uses an authenticator app (TOTP). SMS codes are not supported. Recovery codes are shown once when you enable it; store them somewhere safe.

## Deleting your account

Settings > Account > Delete account. Deletion removes your profile and any applications not currently in `screening`. Reports that were already shared with a landlord are retained for 90 days as required by our landlord agreements, then deleted.

## Support policy

Support agents never ask for your password, full card number, or Social Security number over chat.
